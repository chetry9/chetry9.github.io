# Creative brief: E-commerce tools, rated (BestDigiTools)

- **File**: `ecommerce-tools-rated-2026-bestdigitools.mp4`, 30s, 1080×1920, 30 fps

- **Style**: follows the mint/teal reference. Light scenes (light gray with sky-blue and mint blobs) alternate with dark scenes (black with teal glow). - **Captions**: a premium glass key-point pill at the bottom, with a kicker chip (THE QUESTION / RANK 05…WINNER / NEXT STEP), karaoke word reveal, and the score highlighted in mint.
