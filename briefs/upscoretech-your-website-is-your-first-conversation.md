# Creative brief: Your Website Is Your First Conversation (UpscoreTech)

- **File**: `upscoretech-your-website-is-your-first-conversation.mp4`, 21.0s (630 frames), 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz
- **Cover (0–1s)**: the supplied 1024×1536 thumbnail, fitted to 9:16 over a blurred, slightly darkened copy of itself (nothing cropped), held for 1 second with silence. The 20s animated video follows from 1.0s, so all timings below are +1s in the final file.
- **Audience**: American small business owners, contractors, salon owners, local service businesses, founders
- **Palette**: deep navy #07152D, near-black #050B16, electric blue #1685F8 as the only accent, white text. The characters keep natural skin tones; their clothing uses the brand blues and neutrals.
- **Type**: Inter Display ExtraBold headlines and SemiBold/Medium support text, with masked word-by-word reveals.
- **100% animated**: flat 2.5D vector characters (head, arm and blink motion), a storefront built from geometric blocks, and animated website UI. No photos and no stock footage.
- **Logo**: the supplied UpscoreTech logo, used unchanged and shown only on the end card. It already contains "BUILD SMARTER. GROW FASTER."

| Time | Scene | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.4s | Navy opening, camera push-in. The owner character lifts geometric blocks that snap into a storefront (awning, OPEN sign, windows light up). "YOU **BUILT** THIS BUSINESS." The storefront outline morphs into a browser window. | You worked hard to build this business. |
| 3.4–7.6s | The owner (left) and a customer holding a phone (right) with the website between them. A blue connection line travels owner → website → customer with a glowing dot; communication waves pulse around the browser; camera pans left → right. "YOUR WEBSITE = YOUR **FIRST CONVERSATION**" | Your website is your first conversation with a customer. |
| 7.6–14.5s | The same website zooms to full size and becomes three sections of one page: **What you offer** (service tiles pop, blue underline draws) → **Who you help** (avatar row, a circular target focuses on one customer while the others blur back) → **How to reach you** (the cursor clicks the blue "Book a Free Quote" button, which turns into "✓ Request sent") | Make three things clear: what you offer, who you help, and how to reach you. |
| 14.5–16.0s | Confusing elements float around ("SALE!!! CLICK HERE", "Lorem ipsum…", "????"), then OFFER → CUSTOMER → CONTACT snap into a clean grid with a glow. "DON'T MAKE PEOPLE **GUESS.**" | Don't make people guess. |
| 16.0–20.0s | A blue sweep, then the logo rises in from a mask, a blue accent line, "FOLLOW FOR PRACTICAL BUSINESS LESSONS", and upscoretech.com | Follow UpscoreTech for practical business lessons. |

## Voice
- **Narrator**: one deep, warm, mature American male voice (Kokoro TTS, a 50/50 blend of `am_michael` and `am_onyx`, about 114 Hz median pitch) at a natural 0.92× pace.
- **Script**: used word for word.
- **Scene 3**: recorded as one natural take. Only the pauses after "what you offer," and "who you help," were lengthened so each phrase lands on its card.

## Sound
- **Music**: a soft cinematic pad opening; a light 96 bpm beat enters in scene 2 and builds through scene 3, then resolves on a final chord.
- **Effects**: block placements, whooshes, UI ticks, a refined button click with a confirmation chime, gentle impacts on headline reveals, a snap-to-grid hit, and a clean end chime.
- **Mix**: the music ducks under the voice; loudness is about −14 LUFS.

## Subtitles
Burned in at y≈1700, below the characters and UI, synced word by word. Keywords are highlighted in electric blue.
