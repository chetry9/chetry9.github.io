# Creative brief: The 15-Second Website Test (UpscoreTech)

- **File**: `upscoretech-15-second-website-test.mp4`, 35.5s, 1080×1920, 30 fps
- **Audience**: HVAC and plumbing company owners
- **Look**: built in real 3D like the SiteGround v2 video.
  - **Background**: deep navy with UpscoreTech blue (#0A74FF), a moving 3D grid floor, light rays and parallax bokeh.
  - **Phone**: one 3D phone carries the whole story. It floats, tilts, scrolls the example website, gets tapped, and turns into the DM screen.
  - **Captions**: large, synced word by word; key words in blue.
- **Example website**: the fictional "ComfortPro Heating & Plumbing". None of the reference companies' sites are shown.
- **No fake reviews**: the reviews section shows only placeholder bars labelled "Your real Google reviews go here". The job photos are simple illustrations labelled AC unit / Water heater / Pipe repair / Furnace.

| Time | On-screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6.3s | THE 15-SECOND WEBSITE TEST · "HVAC & Plumbing Owners: Check This!" · phone on screen from the first frame · 15s stopwatch counts down | Own an HVAC or plumbing company? Before spending more money on ads, check your website on your phone. |
| 6.3–10.8s | **1. One-Tap Call Button?** Call Now pulses and is highlighted, a hand taps it, the phone shows a "ComfortPro calling…" screen; chip "✓ Call in one tap" | Can customers call you in one tap? |
| 10.8–15.8s | **2. Clear Service Areas?** Page scrolls to "Areas We Serve", map pins drop, Dallas / Plano / Frisco / Richardson chips light up; chip "✓ Cities up front" | Can they immediately see which cities you serve? |
| 15.8–20.9s | **3. Real Reviews & Job Photos?** Reviews section, then the Recent Jobs gallery; chip "✓ Real proof" | And can they find real customer reviews, or completed job photos? |
| 20.9–26.3s | "Hard to find = fewer calls": the phone desaturates, chips appear ("✕ Call button hidden", "✕ No cities listed", "✕ Reviews buried") and a CALLS line trends down | If those things are hard to find, you may be making it harder for visitors to contact you. |
| 26.3–33.6s | **Get 3 Website Fixes** · DM "CHECK" + Website + City. The phone becomes a DM to UpscoreTech: "CHECK", "comfortprohvac.com", "Dallas, TX", then the reply "We'll take a look 👍" | Want three practical improvements… DM UpscoreTech the word CHECK, along with your website link and city. |
| 33.6–35.5s | UpscoreTech logo + upscoretech.com | We'll take a look. |

## Audio
- **Voice**: male American narrator (Kokoro TTS `am_michael`, 1.05× speed).
- **Music**: an upbeat 110 bpm tech bed that ducks under the voice and drops back during the "hard to find" part.
- **Sound effects**: stopwatch ticks, a tap click with a ringing tone, scroll whooshes, pin pops, chimes, a warning thud with a falling tone, message-send pops, and a logo impact.
- **Loudness**: about −14 LUFS.
