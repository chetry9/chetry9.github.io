# Creative brief: 5 free marketing ideas for US small businesses (UpscoreTech)

- **File**: `upscoretech-5-free-marketing-ideas.mp4`, 37.6s, 1080×1920, 30 fps
- **Audience**: US small business owners
- **Style**: follows the supplied SaaS explainer reference.
  - **Problem (gold)**: black with gold light glows, a timeline line with a glowing dot and month ticks, and a large gold arc sweep.
  - **Transition**: a pixel-block glitch with RGB fringes into the teal palette.
  - **Teal section**: deep navy-black (#00081a) with drifting teal aurora blobs (#19d6ae), a faint grid and sparks.
  - **3D icons**: thick glossy glass icons (pin, star, play, handshake, mail) built as true extruded 3D and spinning.
  - **UI panels**: dark UI with teal borders, tilted in 3D.
  - **Text**: `[ bracket ]` sub-lines, inline teal pill keywords inside sentences, and letter-spacing reveals.
  - **End**: a huge blurred "FREE" zooms through on the way in, and a teal "Follow" button gets a cursor click at the end.
- **Type**: Poppins 600/700.

## Audio
- **Voice**: male American narrator (Kokoro TTS `am_michael`, 1.1× speed).
- **Music**: an energetic 124 bpm four-on-the-floor bed with sidechain pump, off-beat bass, claps and synth stabs, ducked under the voice.
- **Sound effects**: a riser and impact into each idea, whooshes, pops, star dings, a scratch on the strike-through, and a logo hit.
- **Loudness**: about −13 LUFS.

## Notes
- All five ideas are genuinely free.
- No invented statistics are shown. The UI mockups use placeholder business data ("Your Business", (555) number).
