# Creative brief — ChatGPT Free is "unlimited" now, and it has ads in Italy

- **Category**: AI tools
- **Why now**: OpenAI announced unlimited text chats for Free and Go on 6 Aug 2026 (PYMNTS). Ads began serving to Free and Go users in 31 European markets, Italy included, on Mon 24 Aug 2026 (OpenAI, "ChatGPT Ads expands across Europe", 18 Aug 2026; Digiday, 19 Aug; AFP via TechXplore).
- **Hook (0–2s)**: "ChatGPT Free is now unlimited. Here's the catch." (good news / hidden catch)
- **Length**: 30s, 1080×1920, 30 fps
- **Style**: built on the reference SaaS promo: dark green background with a mint glow, orbit rings with logo badges, a large glowing sphere with a cluster of logo badges, kinetic word-by-word type with highlighted words, a tilted floating UI with a glowing border, a word cloud, one big glowing word, and a URL typed into a glowing band.
- **Logos used (original colours)**: ChatGPT, OpenAI, EU, Italy flag, BestDigiTools.

## Voice-over
Male American narrator (Kokoro TTS, voice `am_michael`, 1.12× speed). EQ'd and compressed; the synth music bed (120 bpm) ducks under the voice; loudness normalised to about −14 LUFS.
